Indian Passport — Step-by-Step

Apply online at the official Passport Seva portal. Total process: ~7–15 days (Tatkaal: 1–3 days).

1

Check your documents

Aadhaar card, birth certificate, address proof (Aadhaar / electricity bill / rent agreement), 1 recent photo, and any name-change proof if applicable.

2

Create account on Passport Seva

Register at passportindia.gov.in with your email & mobile. Keep them handy for OTPs.

3

Fill the application & pay

Fill the online form exactly as your documents say (no short names!). Pay the fee online — the current fee is shown on the portal. Choose Tatkaal if you need it urgently.

4

Book your appointment

Book a slot at the nearest Passport Seva Kendra (PSK) or Post Office Passport Seva Kendra (POPSK) on the same portal.

5

Visit PSK with originals

Carry originals + photocopies of every document. Biometrics (photo + fingerprints) are taken on the spot. You'll get an acknowledgment receipt.

6

Police verification & delivery

Police verification happens at your address (be available!). Track status on the portal — passport is delivered by post or SMS pickup.

💡 For Gulf workers (ECR check)

If you belong to the ECR (Emigration Check Required) category — usually passports without a 10th-pass mark sheet — you need emigration clearance to work in ECR countries like UAE, Saudi, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman & Bahrain. Check your passport's ECR status and clear it before flying. Verify rules on the Ministry of External Affairs / mha.gov.in.

🛡️ Avoid these mistakes

  • Names must match Aadhaar / school records exactly
  • Never use agents for the form — it's free and easy
  • Don't pay anyone "extra" for faster delivery
  • Old passport + address change → apply for reissue, not fresh

Student Corner — Your Roadmap

From offer letter to boarding pass — the honest, complete path.

1

Offer letter first

Get your admission & offer letter from the university/college. This is the foundation of every student visa.

2

Money & language proof

Show funds (bank statements, education loan, sponsors) and language scores if the university requires them (IELTS/TOEFL/PTE).

3

Apply on the official portal

Apply for your student visa only on the official portal of your country (see links above). Biometrics usually at a visa centre in your city.

✈️ After the visa — fly smart

Book a one-way student fare with extra baggage allowance. Send us your route and we'll find the student-rate tickets for your intake date — usually before you even start packing.

🎒 Pre-departure checklist

  • Health / travel insurance for the first weeks
  • Forex + international debit/credit card
  • Certificates: passport, visa, offer letter, IELTS
  • Photos, medicines with prescriptions, adapters
