Cabin classes — what you actually get

💺 Economy · illustration

  • Standard seat, 1 checked bag typical (15–23 kg, varies)
  • Meals on full-service; buy-on-board on budget lines
  • Cheapest way to fly — the fare we hunt for you

🛋️ Premium Economy · illustration

  • Wider seat, more legroom, better meals
  • Priority check-in, extra baggage (usually +1 bag)
  • Costs ~1.5–2× economy — good for long flights

💼 Business Class · illustration

  • Flat-bed seat, lounge access, priority everything
  • Multi-course dining, amenity kits, fast immigration lanes
  • Costs ~3–6× economy — our cut suggestion rises with it (+25–55%)

👑 First / Suites · illustration

  • Private suite with door, shower on some A380s
  • Chauffeur transfers, fine-dining on demand
  • The most exclusive way to fly — worth every rupee if you can

🚆 Indian Railways — Every Class Explained

From Sleeper to Vande Bharat — pick the right class for your budget.

🛏️ Sleeper (SL) — most budget, 3-tier open berths, no AC. Ideal for overnight short-mid trips.
❄️ 3 AC (3A) — AC 3-tier berths, curtains, ~2× sleeper price. Most popular overnight class.
💎 2 AC (2A) — AC 2-tier, 4 berths/cabin, more privacy, ~3× sleeper.
👑 1 AC (1A) — AC 1st class, 2-berth lockable cabins, ~5× sleeper. Best comfort on classics.
🪑 Chair Car (CC) — AC seated, day trains like Shatabdi/Tejas. Great for 4–8 hour journeys.
⚡ Vande Bharat — India's flagship semi-high-speed: 160 km/h, AC Chair Car + Executive, onboard meals, fastest city links.
🛤️ Rajdhani / Shatabdi / Duronto — premium express services: fully AC, meals included, priority on the network.
🎟️ Tatkal & Premium Tatkal — last-minute quota opened 1 day before departure at higher fares. We book these too.
🧓 Concession quotas — senior citizens, students, ladies & disabled get fare concessions. Mention it in your request!

🕐 Airport & Flight Basics

Typical rules — always check your airline's latest terms.

Arrive at the airport

  • Domestic: 2 hours before departure (check-in closes ~45–60 min before).
  • International: 3 hours before (immigration + security queues).
  • Peak season / festivals: add 30–60 min more.
  • Gate closes 15–20 min before departure — boarding pass ≠ board.

Typical flying times (examples)

  • Mumbai → Delhi: ~2h05m · Mumbai → Dubai: ~3h15m
  • Delhi → London: ~8h30m · Delhi → New York: ~15h
  • Delhi → Singapore: ~6h · Mumbai → Doha: ~3h40m
  • Bangkok → anywhere in Asia: usually 4–8h

Flight frequencies (typical)

  • Metro-to-metro (Delhi–Mumbai): 30–60+ flights daily, every 15–30 min.
  • Metro-to-tier-2 (Mumbai–Patna): 5–15 flights daily.
  • International hubs (DXB, DOH): 2–8 daily per route from metros.
  • Trains: popular routes run 3–10 services daily; some routes once daily.

Baggage (typical allowances)

  • Domestic economy: 15 kg check-in + 7 kg cabin (full-service).
  • International economy: 23–30 kg (varies by airline/route).
  • Students often get +10–23 kg extra — ask us, we book student fares.
  • Liquids in cabin: max 100 ml per container.

🎓 Study Abroad Compass

Courses, countries and what it takes — figures are typical ranges; always verify on official sites.

🩺 MBBS Abroad

Popular: Russia, China, Philippines, Nepal, Bangladesh, Kyrgyzstan, Georgia. Duration ~5–6 years (incl. internship). Typical annual tuition roughly ₹2–8 lakh (many countries cheaper than Indian private colleges). Requires: NEET, 10+2 with PCB, passport. After graduation, FMGE/NExT to practise in India. Verify the university is in the NMC-recognised list!

⚙️ Engineering

Top picks: Germany (mostly low/no tuition, need blocked account), USA, Canada, Australia, UK. Duration 3–4 years (bachelor) / 1–2 years (master). Requires: strong maths/physics, IELTS/TOEFL, SAT/GRE (varies), funds proof. Germany + Netherlands give the best value for Indian engineers today.

💼 MBA

Top picks: USA (GMAT/GRE), UK, Canada, Australia, Singapore, India (IIMs/CAT). Duration 1–2 years. Requires: bachelor's, work experience (2–5 yrs for top schools), GMAT/CAT, IELTS/TOEFL, essays + interviews. ROI is highest in the US & India's top IIMs.

🌐 Other in-demand courses

Nursing (Australia, UK, Canada, Ireland — high demand), Data Science / IT (Canada, Germany, Australia), Aviation (UK, USA), Culinary (Switzerland, France), Film (UK, Canada). Check each country's skilled-occupation list — it affects your job chances after study.

📄 Universal document checklist

Passport (6+ months validity) · Offer letter · Funds proof (bank statements / education loan / GIC) · Language test (IELTS/TOEFL/PTE) · Entry test (NEET/GMAT/GRE/SAT as applicable) · Photographs · Health/TB certificate if required · Prior academic transcripts. Apply for the student visa only on the official portal — see our Visa Guide.

🕋 Hajj & Umrah — Documents & Steps

We don't arrange pilgrimages — but here's exactly what you need to do it right (verify current rules on the official platforms).

📄 Documents checklist

  • Passport valid 6+ months with blank pages
  • Saudi visa — via official channels only (Hajj: through approved Hajj committees/agents registered with the Ministry of Hajj; Umrah: e-visa/visa-on-arrival rules change — check current policy)
  • Meningococcal (ACWY) vaccination certificate — mandatory, issued 10+ days before travel, valid 3 years
  • Other vaccinations per current Saudi/India health advisories (polio, COVID rules change)
  • Return ticket & confirmed hotel/package booking
  • Funds/QR code for the Nusuk platform (official booking system)
  • Women under 45: check current mahram requirements

🧭 Steps & timing

  • Hajj: happens in Dhul-Hijjah (dates shift ~11 days earlier each year). Book 6–12 months ahead via approved channels.
  • Umrah: year-round, usually 1–3 weeks stay. Book through the official nusuk.sa platform.
  • Visa platforms: visa.mofa.gov.sa (official).
  • Fly via Jeddah (JED) or Madinah (MED) — we book your flights with generous baggage for Ihram essentials.
⚠️ Note: fares, timings, baggage rules, fees and visa rules change frequently. Everything above is typical guidance. Always verify on official airline, railway, university and government websites. Aurelia Tours & Travels books flights & trains — we don't process visas or pilgrimages.
